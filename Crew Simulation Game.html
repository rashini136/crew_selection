🚀 How to Navigate the Rocket

1. Look at the glowing rocket.
The orange highlighted planet/station is your current location.

2. Choose the next route.
Use the route buttons below the map. Each option shows the destination, travel months, and risk level.

3. Click the route button.
The rocket moves automatically. Oxygen reduces by the number of travel months.

4. Resolve the event.
If the route has a hazard, the game flips the coin. Then the location scenario updates oxygen, armour, crew health, and mission time.

White dashed path = normal route Red dashed path = hazard route Finish target: reach Mars Colony in under 9 months
